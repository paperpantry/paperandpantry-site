Privacy Policy

Paper & Pantry Co.
Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

Paper & Pantry Co. (“we,” “us”) operates paperandpantryco.com, an informational website that connects visitors to our products on third-party marketplaces and our social media pages. We keep data collection to a minimum. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not run advertising or tracking on this website.

What we collect

Contact form. If you send us a message through our contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide. We use this only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing.

Website analytics. We use a privacy-focused analytics service to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It reports aggregate information such as pages visited, referring sites, country, and device type.

We do not create user accounts, process payments, or sell products directly on this website.

Service providers

We use third-party service providers to operate this website, including services that host the site, handle contact-form submissions, provide analytics, and help prevent spam. These providers process information only to perform those services for us, and they may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses.

Our contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam. reCAPTCHA may collect information such as your IP address and browser details, and its use is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Social media accounts and automation tools

We use third-party tools and services, including automated and AI-assisted tools, to help manage our social media accounts. These tools may create, schedule, and publish content, review account analytics, and read and respond to messages and comments we receive through those platforms. They access this information through the platforms’ official interfaces, and only for our own accounts.

If you contact us through a social media platform, we may process the information in your message or comment, such as your name or username, in order to respond to you. We use it only for that purpose. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising. Our use of each platform is also subject to that platform’s own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party marketplaces

Our products are offered through third-party marketplaces and online retail platforms. Purchases, accounts, payments, and customer service on those platforms are handled by the platform, not by this website, and their own privacy policies apply. We do not control how those platforms collect, use, or share your information. Please review the privacy policy of any marketplace before you browse or buy there.

Social media

This website may link to our pages on social media platforms. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

How we protect and retain information

We keep messages you send us only as long as needed to respond to you and for ordinary business records, and we don’t share them with anyone except the service providers described above who help deliver them. You may ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us at the address below.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
